The Clarity Method

Translate, Test, Teach.

A five-step method that keeps AI practical, measured, and in your team's hands — never a black box, and never a bet on a tool you don't understand.

  1. 1

    Translate

    Make AI understandable in plain English, for your business specifically.

  2. 2

    Map

    Find the 3 highest-leverage workflows — the jobs worth fixing first.

  3. 3

  4. 4
